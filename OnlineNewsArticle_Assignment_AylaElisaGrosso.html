West Nile, the first death in Genoa

West Nile, Dengue e TBE aren't a distant problem anymore: during summer there's the risk of faster spread.

The fact

On October 9th, 2026 an 86 year old died at San Martino Hospital after contracting West Nile, virus transmitted from mosquitos. It is the second case in genoa: the other one is a 66 year old still in recovery from complications.


What is the West Nile virus?

The West Nile virus is mainly transmitted to humans by mosquitos, especially Culex. Most of the people don't have any symptoms, but 20% can develop fever, headaches, joint pain, nausea and skin rashes.


The consequences

Those cases led to the immediate disinfestation of the zones of Sampierdarena, Sestri Ponente and Voltri. Moreover, this raises the probability of contracting the virus, given the fact that, before, all the cases were during trips abroad or in other regions. The higher temperatures predicted for this autumn also may lead to the increase in cases, especially from mosquitos like Aedes Japonicus and Aedes Koreicus which can resist even lower temperatures.
